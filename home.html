Fresh tacos made with fire and flavor

Where every bite feels like a fiesta.

Taco Tamu is a colorful neighborhood taco shop serving warm tortillas, juicy fillings, crisp toppings, and sauces that wake up your taste buds. From the first crunch to the final drizzle of salsa, every taco is made to be bold, fresh, and unforgettable.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick lunch, meeting friends after school, or grabbing dinner on a busy evening, our tacos bring comfort, energy, and a little celebration to your day.
